Before you ask.

The questions that usually come up on the first call, answered before it.

What does a project cost?

Most projects start around $3,000 CAD. Scope and price are agreed in writing before we start, and if the scope changes you see the cost first and get to say no. No hourly surprises.

How long does it take?

Typically 2–6 weeks from kickoff, depending on scope. We scope the work first — once we have agreed exactly what is being built, you get a firm finish date in writing before any work starts.

Can I edit it myself afterwards?

That depends on what we build. Where a content-managed setup is the right fit we hand over an admin panel and walk you through it, so the day-to-day changes are yours to make. On more custom builds some changes are better kept with us — and we will tell you which is which before we start, not after. Either way, you are never locked out of your own system.

What happens after launch?

Two weeks of post-launch support are included — anything broken, or not behaving the way we agreed, is fixed inside that window at no extra cost. After that, ongoing support moves to a monthly rate scoped to what you actually need. Either way the people maintaining it are the people who built it.

Who actually does the work?

We do — in Toronto. No offshore contractors, no subcontracted build, no account manager relaying your changes to someone you will never meet. The person who takes your call is the person who writes the code.

What happens on the call?

Fifteen minutes. You tell us what you sell and who buys it; we tell you whether we’re the right people for it and roughly what it would cost. If we’re not the right fit we say so on that call — there is no follow-up sequence.
