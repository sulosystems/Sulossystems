AI and automation for Brooklyn small businesses

Most small businesses do not need an AI strategy. They need fewer missed enquiries, less double entry and a quicker way to quote, book and follow up, whether that is a café answering the same booking questions all day, a studio chasing class sign-ups or a contractor losing quotes in the inbox.

Our AI consulting starts with how your business actually runs: we find the pain points that cost the most time and tell you honestly what is worth automating, whether that is plain automation, a connected set of tools or an AI agent. Read the full guide to AI digital transformation for small business, or see what agentic AI can do.

Local search across Brooklyn

Brooklyn customers search by neighbourhood as much as by trade: “bakery Carroll Gardens”, “plumber Park Slope”, “yoga studio Williamsburg”. Google answers with a map and a few listings above the normal results, so your Google Business Profile, your reviews and a website that confirms the same details decide who gets the call.

We set that foundation up properly: the right categories and services on your profile, clear service and neighbourhood pages on your site, and call, booking and direction tracking so you can see which searches bring in customers. We do not guarantee rankings, because no one honestly can. We agree scope in writing and report each month on what changed.

Questions we get asked

Is Sulo Systems based in Brooklyn?

We are a Toronto company, with offices in East York and Woodbridge, and we spend a lot of time in Brooklyn, where our base is in the Carroll Gardens area. We do not run a public storefront there, so we meet clients at their business, over coffee or on video. Book a call and we will arrange a time and place.

Which parts of Brooklyn do you work in?

All of it. That includes Carroll Gardens and its neighbours Cobble Hill, Boerum Hill, Gowanus and Red Hook, plus Park Slope, Brooklyn Heights, Williamsburg, Greenpoint, Bushwick, Bed-Stuy, Crown Heights and beyond.

What kinds of businesses do you help?

Small, owner-led businesses that depend on being found and booked by people nearby: shops, restaurants and cafés, studios and gyms, trades and contractors, and creative and professional services.

How much does it cost?

Scope and price are agreed in writing before any work starts, and new projects start at $3,000. We agree payment terms at the same time, so there are no surprises. A 15-minute call is enough to give you a rough number.

Do you work with businesses elsewhere in New York?

Yes. Most of the work happens over video and shared documents, so we also take on businesses in the other boroughs and across New York. Toronto, Brooklyn and Raleigh all share the Eastern time zone, so calls fit normal business hours.

Do you help small businesses with AI and automation?

Yes. We start with an AI consulting assessment of how your business runs, find the pain points that cost the most time and tell you what is worth automating. If you want it built, we build it in small stages that prove themselves, and we say so plainly when AI is not the answer.

Do you build booking or restaurant software?

Yes. We have built our own products: Tempo for bookings, classes and scheduling, and Table Ready for restaurant reservations and waitlists. We can also build custom software around how your business already works.

Want to talk about your Brooklyn business?

Tell us what you do and who you serve. In 15 minutes we will say what we would do first, whether we are the right people, and roughly what it would cost. If we are not the right fit, we will say so on the call.
