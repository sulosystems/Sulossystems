The starting point

An outfitter with many trips, seasons and group types gets a steady stream of questions before anyone books: which trip suits a family, what is included, when to go, what to bring. Some arrive in the evening or at the weekend, when no one is there to answer, and a question left until morning can be a booking that went elsewhere.

The aim with Quinn was to answer where the guest is asking, at the moment they ask, and keep the conversation moving toward a booking rather than a contact form to fill in and wait on.

What Quinn does

Kept deliberately narrow: one job, done properly.

Answers on the page

Guests ask a question in their own words and get an answer straight away, without leaving the page or waiting for an email.

Works when the office is closed

Evenings, weekends and holidays are covered, which is when many enquiries arrive.

Moves toward a booking

The conversation is aimed at helping the guest decide and book, not only at answering and stopping.

How we keep an agent like Quinn safe

An AI agent talking to customers on your behalf has to be designed carefully. The principles we build agents on are the same ones we describe on our agentic AI page: one narrowly defined job, the least access that does the work, a person approving anything costly or hard to undo, and a start in a mode where the agent only suggests until it has proved itself on real cases.

Just as important is a clear hand-off. An agent that is unsure should say so and bring in a person, rather than guess.

Questions we get asked

What is Quinn?

Quinn is Sulo Systems’ AI agent. At Voyageur Quest it answers guest enquiries on the page in real time and moves them toward a booking.

Can Quinn work for my business?

Yes. Quinn can be set up as an AI receptionist for service businesses, answering questions, qualifying enquiries and booking into your calendar. See our AI receptionist page for how it works.

Does an AI agent replace staff?

No. It handles the first response and the routine questions so your team can focus on the conversations that need a person, and it hands over when it is unsure.

Is it safe to let an AI agent talk to customers?

It can be, with the right design: a narrow job, limited access, approval steps for anything risky, a clear hand-off to a person and a period where the agent only suggests. We design those in from the start.
