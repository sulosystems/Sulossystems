How we work

Onshore, end to end

Designed, built and maintained by our own team in Toronto. The person who takes your call is the person who changes the page.

Fixed scope, in writing

Scope and price are agreed before we start. If the scope changes, you see the cost first and get to say no. New projects start at $3,000 CAD.

Two weeks of support

Anything broken or not behaving the way we agreed is fixed in the two weeks after launch at no extra cost. After that, support is a monthly rate scoped to your needs.
