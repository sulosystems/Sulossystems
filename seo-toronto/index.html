SEO that is measured by enquiries

A report full of rising impressions is easy to produce and does not pay your bills. We set up tracking first, so that calls, form submissions and bookings are counted, and then we work backwards from them: which pages bring in enquiries, which searches they come from, and what stands between the next customer and your contact form.

That changes the work. Less time on vanity keywords, more on the pages and fixes that move real enquiries.

What is included

The work depends on what your audit finds. This is the full menu.

Technical SEO

Crawling and indexing, page speed, mobile usability, site structure, redirects, broken links and structured data. The plumbing that decides whether Google can read your site properly.

On-page SEO

Titles, headings, descriptions and page copy that match what your customers type, written to read well for a person first.

Content strategy

A plan for the pages and guides that answer your customers’ real questions, prioritized by how likely they are to bring in work.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile, local pages and reviews for businesses that serve a place. See our local SEO service for the detail.

AI search visibility

Making your business easy for AI assistants and Google’s AI answers to find and quote: clear direct answers, structured data, consistent facts and content written to be cited.

Earned authority

Links and mentions from real sources: directories that matter, partners, local organizations and press. We do not buy links or use link schemes.

Tracking and reporting

Google Analytics 4, Search Console and tag management set up properly, with a plain monthly report on what changed and what it produced.

How we work

  1. Audit

    A technical and content review of your site, your search presence and your competitors, ending in a short prioritized list.

  2. Roadmap

    We agree what to do first and why, with scope and price in writing before any work starts.

  3. Fix and publish

    Technical fixes and on-page changes go live first. We can make them ourselves on your site or hand a clear brief to your developer.

  4. Build content

    New and improved pages, one at a time, each with a clear job and a clear next step.

  5. Measure and adjust

    Monthly reporting tied to enquiries, then the next month’s plan based on what the numbers say.

Why work with a team that builds websites

Most SEO agencies write a recommendations document and wait for someone else to implement it. We design, build and maintain websites and software, so the same people who find the problem can fix it. That is faster, and nothing gets lost between an agency, a developer and whoever runs your ads.

If your site needs more than fixes, we can rebuild it with SEO designed in from the first page rather than bolted on afterwards.

Questions we get asked

How much do your SEO services cost?

It depends on the size of the site, your market and how much needs doing. We agree scope and price in writing before work starts, and ongoing work is a monthly rate scoped to what you need. A 15-minute call is enough to give you a rough number.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical and on-page fixes can show effects within weeks. Competitive searches usually take months. We report monthly so you can see progress long before it shows up in your phone.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody controls Google. We commit to clear scope, honest reporting and the work itself, and we show results in enquiries so you are not asked to trust a promise.

Do you do link building?

We earn links and mentions from real sources: relevant directories, partners, local organizations and press. We do not buy links or use schemes that put your site at risk.

Is SEO better than Google Ads?

They do different jobs. Ads bring traffic while you pay for it; SEO builds visibility that keeps working. One of our clients rebuilt their site and SEO, switched their paid ads off, and the calls did not stop. Many businesses use both, and we can help you decide the mix.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Often not. Many sites need fixes rather than a rebuild. The audit tells us which, and we will say so plainly if a rebuild is the better investment.

What is AI search optimization?

It is making your business easy for AI assistants and AI answers in search to find and cite. That means clear direct answers on your pages, structured data, consistent facts about your business across the web and content worth quoting. It builds on good traditional SEO rather than replacing it.
