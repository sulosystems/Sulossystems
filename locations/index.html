Why two offices

East York puts us in central and east Toronto. Woodbridge puts us in Vaughan and the northwest GTA, where many small and medium businesses sit well away from downtown agencies. Whichever is closer to you, you get the same services and the same fixed-scope way of working.

In practice most of the work happens over the phone, on video and in shared documents, so distance rarely matters. The offices give you a real local address to find us at, and a team that knows the areas you do business in.

Questions we get asked

Where are Sulo Systems’ offices?

We have two: 2 Grandstand Pl in East York, Toronto, and 681 Chancellor Dr in Woodbridge, Vaughan. Both can be reached on (647) 524-6974.

Which areas do you serve?

Toronto (including East York and North York), Vaughan and Woodbridge, Mississauga and the wider Greater Toronto Area. Most of the work is done remotely, so we also take on businesses elsewhere.

Do I have to come to an office?

No. Most first conversations are a 15-minute phone or video call. If you would rather meet in person, say so when you book and we will arrange it.

Do you charge differently by location?

No. Scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts, and new projects start at $3,000 CAD, wherever you are.

Not sure which office is closest?

Tell us what you do and who you serve. In 15 minutes we will say what we would do first, whether we are the right people, and roughly what it would cost. If we are not the right fit, we will say so on the call.
