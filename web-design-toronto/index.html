Design that does a job

Most small business websites fail in the same few ways. They are slow on a phone, they say what the owner cares about rather than what the customer needs, and the next step is buried. None of that is a design-taste problem. It is a clarity problem, and clarity is what we design for.

Every page we build starts with one question: what should a visitor do next, and what do they need to believe first? The layout, the copy and the proof all follow from the answer.

What you get

Every site we build includes these as standard.

Mobile first

Designed on a phone first, then scaled up, because most of your visitors arrive on one.

Fast

Lightweight pages and optimized images, because speed affects both your visitors and how Google treats your site.

SEO built in

Clear titles and headings, a clean structure, structured data and sensible URLs from the first page, so you are not paying to fix it later.

Built to convert

Booking, quote and phone buttons where visitors look for them, with your enquiries tracked so you know what is working.

Accessible

Readable contrast, keyboard navigation and proper labels. It is the right thing to do and it helps everyone.

Yours to run

Where an editable setup fits, we hand over an admin panel and show you how to use it. Where it does not, we tell you up front.

How a design project runs

  1. A 15-minute call

    You tell us what you sell and who buys it. We tell you whether we are the right people and roughly what it costs.

  2. Scope in writing

    Pages, features, price and a firm finish date are agreed in writing before work starts.

  3. Design and copy

    We draft the structure and the words together, so the design fits the message instead of the other way round.

  4. Build and review

    You see working pages early and often, and we adjust before anything goes live.

  5. Launch and support

    We launch, set up tracking and search tools, and include two weeks of post-launch support.

Redesigns without losing your rankings

If you already have a site that brings in some traffic, a redesign has to protect it. We map every existing page to its new home, set up the right redirects and keep the titles and content that already work, so you gain a better site without dropping out of search.

New projects start at $3,000 CAD, with scope and price agreed in writing, and typical timelines are 2 to 6 weeks from kickoff.

Questions we get asked

How much does a website cost?

New projects start at $3,000 CAD. The final price depends on the pages, features and content, and it is agreed in writing before we start. On a 15-minute call we can usually give you a rough number.

How long does it take?

Typically 2 to 6 weeks from kickoff, depending on scope. You get a firm finish date in writing once we have agreed exactly what is being built.

Will my new website be good for SEO?

Yes. Titles, headings, structure, speed and structured data are part of every build, so the site is ready to be found. For ongoing growth in search we also offer SEO and local SEO services.

Can I update the site myself?

Where a content-managed setup fits we hand over an admin panel and walk you through it. On more custom builds some changes are better kept with us, and we tell you which is which before we start.

Can you redesign my existing site without hurting my rankings?

Yes. We map old pages to new ones, set up redirects and keep the content that already performs, so rankings are protected through the change.

Do you build online stores?

Yes. We built a premium e-commerce brand site for a high-ticket appliance retailer, and we can build and connect stores, payments and inventory.

Do you work on WordPress or Wix sites?

Our builds are custom-coded, but we also work on sites our clients already have. We have done SEO and improvements on both WordPress and Wix sites.
